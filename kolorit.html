This is where the color comes forward. Three pre‑tuned bands lift quiet detail and hold peaks in place, upward and downward at once.

In doing so they pull up the harmonics the saturation has just created, so the color gets richer and more present.

UP and DOWN set each direction, TIME shapes the response and the MIX ring blends it all in parallel.

Warmth and body on Soft, glue and weight on Tape, edge and bite on Hard. Each comes in a clean and an ANALOG version, six characters in total. LOW keeps the sub tight while the bass gains presence.

Noise that moves with your signal. It follows every hit of the original signal and falls silent in the gaps, so it never turns into hiss. White, Tape and Vinyl, each with or without ANALOG.

Brighter or warmer with one knob. A clean slope around 800 Hz, free of resonances and without any color of its own. If saturation and dynamics tip the sound too far, Tilt brings it back.

Grit
Click the Grit lettering and the dynamics turn aggressive: faster detection, a harder knee, tails pulled right up. Made for basses that need to growl and drums that need to get dense.
Analog
The whole chain starts behaving like hardware. Left and right get slightly different component values, the dynamics recover program-dependently and Tape picks up a hint of wow and flutter. On Vinyl, a virtual record spins at 33⅓ rpm.
HQ
4x oversampling for the saturation instead of 2x. Highs stay clean and free of aliasing, even when you push the drive hard. HQ works live and in the export.
Output Mix
The global MIX in the output section blends the whole processed chain with your dry signal, latency- and phase-aligned.
Workflow
82 presets in 17 categories, from kicks and bass to vocals, buses and full mixes. Two independent A/B settings for instant comparison and 30 steps of undo.
Auto Gain
Louder always seems better, which makes honest decisions hard. Auto Gain measures loudness the way a LUFS meter does and keeps the output at the loudness of your original signal, even when you crank INPUT, so you only hear the change in color. To let KOLORIT add level, switch it off.
